The plays, ranked

Ordered by return on the money already being spent.

01

Turn on Commerce for wholesale ordering

Paid, unused

This is the single biggest gap. You are paying $52.25 a month for invoices, quotes, payment links and subscriptions, and sending wholesale buyers to Faire instead — where a commission is taken on every order. HubSpot Commerce lets a buyer pay you directly.

  • Build a reusable quote template for a standard wholesale case order.
  • Send payment links in the B-track follow-ups so a buyer can order without leaving the email.
  • Use subscriptions for recurring standing orders — the natural fit for a store that reorders monthly.
  • Keep Faire for discovery of new stockists; move repeat buyers onto direct invoicing.
02

Run wholesale accounts through a deal pipeline

Paid, unused

You just emailed 204 prospects. Right now there is nowhere for a reply to live except an inbox. A pipeline turns “interested” into a tracked stage with a value and a next step, and tells you which of the three follow-ups is actually producing revenue.

  • Stages that match reality: Enquiry → Samples sent → First order → Reordering.
  • Attach the deal to the company, not just the person — buyers change jobs.
  • Forecast by stage so you know how much wholesale is genuinely in flight.
03

Capture leads with forms and a meetings link

Included, unused

Sales Hub Starter includes a scheduling link and Marketing Starter includes forms. The wholesale page currently asks people to email. A booking link converts far better than an invitation to compose a message.

  • Put a “Book 15 minutes” link on the wholesale page and in the B3 final follow-up.
  • Replace the contact form on the announcement page with a HubSpot form so submissions create contacts automatically.
  • Every submission lands in the CRM already segmented — no manual list building.
04

Connect Shopify to HubSpot

Included, unused

Data Hub Starter plus 625,000 daily API calls, and the two systems currently know nothing about each other. Order history is what makes segmentation good rather than generic.

  • Sync customers and orders so “Previous Buyers” maintains itself instead of being hand-built.
  • Segment on real behaviour: last order date, flavours bought, order value.
  • Abandoned-cart and post-purchase emails become possible because the data is finally there.
05

Use the shared inbox for wholesale support

Included, unused

Service Hub Starter gives a shared conversations inbox. When B3 asks “Should I close your file?” and replies arrive, they should be visible to whoever is handling wholesale, attached to the contact record — not sitting in one person's mail.
