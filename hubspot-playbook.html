Systems Report

Emails went out August 26. Then two systems stopped.

The first two funnel emails reached 255 people on the night of August 26. Since then the HubSpot account has been locked for non-payment, so the three follow-ups have not gone out, and the social calendar ran dry on August 20 with nothing queued behind it. Everything below is read from the live accounts today.

HubSpot is locked. The $66.25 that was due August 19 is still unpaid, and HubSpot has moved from a warning banner to a full lock: “Your account is temporarily locked because of outstanding payments.” No emails can be sent, and the open and click reports for the August 26 sends cannot be opened until it is paid. The follow-ups that were due August 31 and September 2 did not go out.

Social posting has been dark for 14 days. The last MOKIPOPS post published on August 20 at 6:00 PM ET. Blotato has zero posts scheduled. Nothing has appeared on Facebook or Instagram since.

What we cannot see yet

Open rate, click rate, replies logged in HubSpot and wholesale form submissions all sit behind the lock. The moment the balance is paid those numbers can be added to this page. Replies to moreflavor@mokipops.com should be checked directly in that mailbox in the meantime.

Did the emails move anything?

Shopify store traffic, July 1 to September 3, compared with the previous 65 days.

Yes, visibly. After the mid-July launch spike, store traffic settled to roughly 30 to 40 sessions a day. The only two spikes since then are August 26 to 30, when daily sessions jumped to roughly 120 to 130 on two separate days. Nothing else changed that week: no posts were going out, no ads were running. That is the email.

  • The announcement page at mokipops.com/pages/watch recorded 33 sessions. It is linked only from the emails, so those are email readers who clicked through to watch the video.
  • The wholesale page on the store recorded 22 sessions, up 175% on the prior period.
  • Add-to-cart rose to 19 (up 46%) and reached checkout rose to 14 (up 56%) versus the prior period.
  • Completed online orders: 0. Fourteen people got as far as checkout and none finished. That is the single most fixable number on this page, and it is covered under next steps.

Attribution is by timing. HubSpot’s own click data will confirm it once the account is unlocked.

Failures worth knowing about

88 scheduled posts failed. 83 of those were on a single day, July 16, when a bulk queue hit Facebook’s limit of 35 posts per page per 24 hours; the remaining five were media that Facebook or Instagram could not process. Nothing failed after August 2. The July 16 pile-up is the reason the calendar was later changed to one post a day.

Social to store

Instagram sent 32 sessions to the store (up 23%), Facebook sent 6, LinkedIn 1. Against 27,854 views that is a click-through well under one percent, which is normal for Instagram because captions are not clickable. Social did its job as awareness. It was never going to be the channel that closes orders, and the numbers say so plainly.

What to do next, in order

01

Pay the $66.25 and unlock HubSpot

Everything else in the email lane is behind this. Account & Billing → Pay now. The next bill of $66.25 lands September 19, so both may be due together.

02

Send the two overdue follow-ups the same day

B2 (the Waymo activation story) to the 204 prospects and A2 (one case for your event) to the 51 past customers. Both are already built and tested; they are a minute each to send. Then B3, “Should I close your file?”, roughly a week later. Pull the open and click numbers for A1 and B1 while in there and add them to this page.

03

Refill the social calendar today

Fourteen dark days after a month of growth is the most expensive gap on this page. One post a day at noon ET, September through October, weighted toward the formats that won: facility and kitchen video, pop-up footage, kids reacting. Layer in fall and Halloween angles. Fewer designed stills.

04

Find out why 14 checkouts became 0 orders

Run a test checkout on the case of 24 from a phone. The usual suspects for frozen goods are a shipping quote that appears late and is larger than the product, or a delivery area check that fails. Whatever it is, the email traffic arrived, found the cart, and left at the last step.

05

Fix the wholesale page on the store

mokipops.com/pages/wholesale returned a 404 today yet still drew 22 visits. Either restore the page or redirect it to the live wholesale page so no buyer hits a dead end.

06

Get brand-portal access to Faire

So page views, follows and retailer messages can sit alongside the order count in the next report.

07

Clear the 49 legacy unfulfilled orders in Shopify

Mark them fulfilled or archive them so the dashboard reflects reality.
