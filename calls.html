📞 Agent Phone Script — MOKIPOPS Wholesale

Goal: land a first case order (or a wholesale account) and always capture an email before hanging up. Pricing below is current and confirmed — don't invent other tiers.

1 · Opening (10 seconds)

"Hi, may I speak with [first name]? … Hey [first name], this is [agent name] calling from MOKIPOPS — the all-natural frozen fruit pops here in Atlanta. You've grabbed our pops before, so this is a quick thank-you call plus something new we're doing. Got 60 seconds?"

If they don't remember: "We're the kid-founded popsicle brand — fresh fruit, no junk — you may have seen us at a local event or market."

2 · The hook

"We just opened up wholesale and event ordering. People who love the pops are stocking them for offices, shops, cafés, gyms, schools, and events — and the per-pop price drops from the $4 retail to about $2 a pop."

3 · The offer (confirmed pricing)

TierPricePer pop
Standard wholesale case (24 pops)$53.50≈ $2.23
Event / activation case (25 pops)$46.00$1.84
Retail single (mokipops.com)$4.00$4.00
  • Retailers: suggested retail is $1.49–$2.49 per pop from distributor pricing — healthy margin on every pop. Big commitments (pallet = 300 cases) get distributor pricing down to $0.80/pop.
  • Events: $46/case of 25, 50% deposit at invoice, balance due 3 business days after the event. Fully-staffed cart service available.

4 · Discovery questions (pick 2)

  • "Do you run or know a business — a shop, café, gym, school, or office — that would move frozen treats in summer?"
  • "Any events coming up — company parties, church functions, school days, markets?"
  • "When you bought from us, what flavor did you get? The lineup's grown since."

5 · Objections

  • "I'm just an individual." → "Totally fine! Most of our wholesale starts with one person who brings a case to work or an event. One case is 24 pops for $53.50 — that's the party sorted."
  • "Too expensive." → "At wholesale you're at ~$2.23 a pop versus $4 retail — and for events the activation case brings it to $1.84. For resale, the margin chain is built in."
  • "Need to think about it." → "No pressure. What's the best email to send the one-page price sheet to?" (Always get the email!)

6 · Close

"Great — I'll get you set up. What's the best email for the invoice and price sheet? … Perfect. And if you ever want ongoing wholesale, we're also on Faire for easy reordering — and everything's at borngifted.github.io/mokipops-reel/wholesale.html, I can text you the link."
